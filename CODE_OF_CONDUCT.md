# Code of conduct

Omnidex follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback on the work rather than the person, and leave harassment, personal attacks and discrimination out of every project space, including issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Umbra-Lab/Omnidex/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct report", or by contacting a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's community impact guidelines.
